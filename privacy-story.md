# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Introduction

HanStories ("知故") is a Chinese story learning app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you register, we collect your name, email address, and an encrypted password. This is the minimum information needed to create your account.

### Learning Data
Your favorites are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your learning data to our servers.

### Subscription Data
If you subscribe to Premium, Apple handles all payment processing. We do not collect or store your payment information. We only receive confirmation of your subscription status from Apple.

### Device Information
We do not collect device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.

## How We Use Your Information

Your account information is used solely for authentication. We do not sell, share, or rent your personal information to any third parties. We do not use your data for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Data Storage & Security

Your password is encrypted using industry-standard hashing before storage. Favorites are stored locally on your device. Server communication uses HTTPS encryption.

## Third-Party Services

### Apple StoreKit
Used for subscription management. Apple's privacy policy applies to payment processing.

### Text-to-Speech
We use Apple's built-in AVSpeechSynthesizer for pronunciation. All speech processing happens on your device.

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Children's Privacy

HanStories is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request deletion of your account and associated data
- Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@hanstories-app.com
